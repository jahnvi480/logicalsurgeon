My Teaching Story

From first-year confusion to final-year confidence, I teach the reasoning behind each surgical step, not just the final answer.

My students often tell me that my sessions are practical, interactive, and easy to retain because I explain surgery through real clinical situations. This helps them answer with confidence in viva, case presentations, and theory papers.

As a professor and surgeon, I have built a learning approach where exam performance and bedside reasoning grow together. Through LogicalSurgeonn and Infusion Batch, I have worked to make surgery approachable for undergraduates across institutions.

When topics feel difficult, I break them into clear logic trees, management pathways, and decision checkpoints so students remember the concept long after the exam.
