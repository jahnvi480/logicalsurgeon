Privacy & medical information

A little clarity.
Before you connect.

I want you to understand what happens when you browse LogicalSurgeonn or prepare an enquiry, and what this website is not intended for.

Website notice updated 5 November 2026. This is an implementation date, not a medical review date.

Your enquiry

A draft.
Not an automatic submission.

The home-page enquiry form uses the details you enter to prepare WhatsApp and email draft links in the current page. It does not submit your enquiry to a website backend or automatically send a message. The website code does not save the form details in custom browser storage.

You choose whether to open a provider, review the draft and send it there. Opening a draft link passes its contents to the selected app or provider, even before you choose to send. Editing a form field invalidates earlier draft links on the page.

Your browser or app may still retain history, autofill information, drafts or sent messages according to its settings and policies. This website’s draft-only flow is not a promise that nothing is collected or retained by those services.

Browsing & external links

Other services.
Their own policies.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and receives network requests when you visit. Google Fonts supplies fonts on these pages. The home page also loads Font Awesome resources from cdnjs and includes a Google Maps iframe that can make requests when it loads. These providers receive network information such as your IP address and may have their own logs, cookies and privacy policies.

The Clinic page uses a directions link rather than an embedded map. Opening directions takes you to Google Maps. WhatsApp, email, social links and external course links take you to other apps or providers; their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

Please review the relevant provider information: GitHub privacy statement, Google privacy policy, WhatsApp privacy policy and Cloudflare privacy policy (cdnjs). Check your email, social or course provider’s policy before sharing information there.

Important limits

Not a medical-records
or emergency service.

Please do not upload or send medical records, reports, prescriptions or other sensitive clinical information through this website or its enquiry links. Keep enquiries to appointment logistics or educational questions.

This website does not provide personal medical advice. Courses and teaching content are for education, not patient care or a substitute for an individual clinical assessment.

For an emergency, seek immediate local emergency care. Do not use the form or wait for an email or WhatsApp reply.

For a planned visit, see Clinic and confirm the full address, hours, availability and fees directly. An enquiry is not an appointment confirmation.
