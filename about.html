The way I teach

Start with the why.

My academic path took me from MBBS at JJ Hospital, Mumbai, to MS in General Surgery at GMC Aurangabad. Alongside my work as a surgeon, teaching is how I bring clinical reasoning into the classroom.

LogicalSurgeonn reflects that approach: take a clinical case, understand the concepts behind it, and work through the management pathway. In my sessions, we explore cases and viva questions together rather than treating surgery as a list of answers to memorise.

For learning links and the Infusion Batch, visit Courses. For my academic writing, explore Publications.
